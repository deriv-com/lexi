How it builds

The spec is the contract.
You own the gates.

01

Spec first, always

Every build starts from a Business Spec. If there isn't one, Lexi writes it with you: a recommendation with every question, every assumption logged. Reviews check the work against the spec, never against taste.

02

Two approvals that are yours

Nothing gets built until you approve the design prototype, and nothing ships until you approve the result. The bridge enforces both in code: Lexi can ask, she cannot approve for you.

03

Review loops that end

A reviewer checks the spec, a tester clicks the journeys. Findings go back to the builder at most three times, then Lexi brings the call to you instead of circling.
